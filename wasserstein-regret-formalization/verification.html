Precisely what was checked

Scope: Sections 2 and 3 of the manuscript, together with their required preliminary and appendix results. Later computational and finite-sample statistical results are outside this verification snapshot. Formalization of the full paper is in progress.

The five main statements are Theorem 2.2, Lemmas 3.1–3.3, and Theorem 3.4. The remaining scoped results supply their preliminaries and appendix proofs. Four cited-support wrappers are also proved.

Reading the bookkeeping classification

The 25 bookkeeping entries make the Euclidean norm explicit: ‖v‖² = vᵀv. This matches the manuscript’s risk and regret formulas. It was classified as a convention, rather than a weakening of a scientific claim.

The Lean statements also use a singular-value convention predicate. Both convention predicates are themselves proved in Lean:

  • assPin1_valid
  • assSingularValueConvention_valid

All estimator guarantees apply to every admissible minimizer selection. Uniqueness is not assumed.

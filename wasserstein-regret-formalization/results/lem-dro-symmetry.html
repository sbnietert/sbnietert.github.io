Lemma C.4 / scoped manuscript result

Averaging over symmetries

Proved in LeanPapers.WassersteinRegretS2S3.droSymmetry

Manuscript statement

Let U be a compact subgroup of
{diag(UX​,UY​):UX​∈Op, UY​∈Om}.
Suppose P,P~∈P2​(Rd) satisfy U♯​P=P and U♯​P~=P~ for every U∈U. Let ε>0, and fix any minimizer A^∈A for the DROε​[P~], MROε​[P~], or SNRε​[P~] optimization problem.Let μ be normalized Haar measure on U and define
Aˉ:=∫U​UY⊤​A^UX​dμ(U).
Then Aˉ is a minimizer of the same optimization problem, is invariant in the sense that UY^⊤AUX=A ∀ U∈ U, and satisfies R(Aˉ,P)≤R(A^,P). Consequently, restricting any of these optimization problems to invariant coefficient matrices preserves its optimal value. Moreover, to lower bound the target regret of every optimizer, it suffices to lower bound the target regret of every invariant optimizer.

Convention made explicit. This result carries the Euclidean-norm convention used by the manuscript. The formalization proves that convention in assPin1_valid. The singular-value convention is proved in assSingularValueConvention_valid. Both are included in the axiom audit.

Formal statement

Exact signature from the verified source. Open the proof to follow each dependency.

lemma droSymmetry {p m : ℕ} [NeZero p] [NeZero m] (hpin : assPin1 m)
    (U : Subgroup (BlockOrth p m)) (hU : assDroSymmetryCompactGroup U)
    (P tildeP : JointLaws p m) (hinv : assDroSymmetryInvariantLaws U P tildeP)
    (eps : ℝ≥0) (o : Fin 4) (Ahat : Mat m p)
    (hmin : assDroSymmetryMinimizer o tildeP eps Ahat) :
    let barA := averageCoefficient U hU Ahat
    barA ∈ populationMinimizers o tildeP eps ∧ invariantCoefficient U barA ∧
    regret barA P ≤ regret Ahat P ∧
    (⨅ A : Mat m p, populationObjective o tildeP eps A) =
      ⨅ (A : Mat m p) (_ : invariantCoefficient U A), populationObjective o tildeP eps A ∧
    (∀ b : ℝ,
      (∀ A ∈ populationMinimizers o tildeP eps, invariantCoefficient U A → b ≤ regret A P) →
      ∀ A ∈ populationMinimizers o tildeP eps, b ≤ regret A P)

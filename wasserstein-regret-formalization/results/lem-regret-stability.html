Regret stability

Proved in LeanPapers.WassersteinRegretS2S3.regretStability

Manuscript statement

For all A∈A, P∈P∗, and Q∈P2​(Rd), we have
​R(A,P)​−R(A,Q)​​≤(2κP​​+∥A∥op​)W2​(P,Q).​
Moreover, for A=AP​, we have R(AP​,Q)≤4κP​W2​(P,Q)2.

Convention made explicit. This result carries the Euclidean-norm convention used by the manuscript. The formalization proves that convention in assPin1_valid. The singular-value convention is proved in assSingularValueConvention_valid. Both are included in the axiom audit.

Formal statement

Exact signature from the verified source. Open the proof to follow each dependency.

lemma regretStability {p m : ℕ} [NeZero p] [NeZero m]
    (hpin : assPin1 m) (hsv : assSingularValueConvention) (A : Mat m p)
    (P : NondegenerateLaws p m) (Q : JointLaws p m) :
    |Real.sqrt (regret A P.carrier.val) - Real.sqrt (regret A Q)| ≤
      (2 * Real.sqrt (conditionNumber P.carrier.val).toReal + opNorm A) * wassersteinDistance
        P.carrier.val Q ∧
    ENNReal.ofReal (regret (optimalPredictor P) Q) ≤
      4 * conditionNumber P.carrier.val * ENNReal.ofReal (wassersteinDistance P.carrier.val Q)
         ^ 2

Lemma A.6 / scoped manuscript result

Row-space projection perturbation

Proved in LeanPapers.WassersteinRegretS2S3.sinThmVariant

Manuscript statement

Let M0​∈Rr×d have full row rank, and let M1​=M0​+Δ for some Δ∈Rr×d. Let Π0​,Π1​∈Rd×d denote orthogonal projections onto the row spaces of M0​,M1​, respectively. Then,
∥(Id​−Π0​)Π1​∥F​≤∥(Id​−Π1​)Π0​∥F​≤σmin​(M0​)∥Δ∥F​​.

Formal statement

Exact signature from the verified source. Open the proof to follow each dependency.

lemma sinThmVariant (hsv : assSingularValueConvention) {r d : ℕ} [NeZero r] [NeZero d]
    (M0 : Mat r d) -- @realizes \bM_0(real reference rectangle)
    (Delta : Mat r d) -- @realizes \bDelta(arbitrary rectangular perturbation)
    (hrank : assSinThmVariantRowRank M0) :
    let M1 := M0 + Delta -- @realizes \bM_1(M0 plus Delta)
    let Pi0 := rowProjector M0 -- @realizes \bPi_0(reference row projector)
    let Pi1 := rowProjector M1 -- @realizes \bPi_1(perturbed row projector)
    frob ((1 - Pi0) * Pi1) ≤ frob ((1 - Pi1) * Pi0) ∧
    frob ((1 - Pi1) * Pi0) ≤ frob Delta / minSV M0

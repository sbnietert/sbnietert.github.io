Lemma B.1 / scoped manuscript result

Regression factor identities and regret formula

Proved in LeanPapers.WassersteinRegretS2S3.regretProjectionFormula

Manuscript statement

Let Σ∈S+d​, and fix any S∈Rd×r such that Σ=SS⊤. Let ΠX​=SX†​SX​ be the orthogonal projection onto the row space of SX​. Then
AΣ​=ΣYX​ΣX†​=SY​SX†​.
For the chosen factor S, the corresponding residual factor
E:=SY​−AΣ​SX​=SY​(I−ΠX​)
satisfies
ESX⊤​=0,
E ΠX=0, E E^⊤=ΣE. Finally, for every A∈A, we have L(A,Σ)=∥ASX​−SY​∥F2​ and
R(A,Σ)=∥(SY​−ASX​)ΠX​∥F2​=∥(A−AΣ​)SX​∥F2​.

Convention made explicit. This result carries the Euclidean-norm convention used by the manuscript. The formalization proves that convention in assPin1_valid. The singular-value convention is proved in assSingularValueConvention_valid. Both are included in the axiom audit.

Formal statement

Exact signature from the verified source. Open the proof to follow each dependency.

lemma regretProjectionFormula {p m r : ℕ} [NeZero p] [NeZero m]
    (hpin : assPin1 m) (hsv : assSingularValueConvention) (Sigma : PSD (p + m))
    (S : Mat (p + m) r) -- @realizes \bS(arbitrary width real Gram factor)
    (hfactor : assRegretProjectionFormulaFactor Sigma S) :
    let SX := (factorBlocks p m S).1 -- @realizes \bS_X(feature rows with width r)
    let SY := (factorBlocks p m S).2 -- @realizes \bS_Y(response rows with width r)
    let PiX := rowProjector SX -- @realizes \bPi_X(factor row projector)
    let E := SY - canonicalCoefficient Sigma.val * SX -- @realizes \bE(optimal residual factor)
    canonicalCoefficient Sigma.val = blockYX p m Sigma.val * moorePenroseInverse (blockX p m
      Sigma.val) ∧
    canonicalCoefficient Sigma.val = SY * moorePenroseInverse SX ∧
    E = SY * (1 - PiX) ∧ E * SX.transpose = 0 ∧ E * PiX = 0 ∧
    E * E.transpose = matrixResidual Sigma.val ∧
    (∀ A : Mat m p, matrixRisk A Sigma.val = frob (A * SX - SY) ^ 2 ∧
      matrixRegret A Sigma.val = frob ((SY - A * SX) * PiX) ^ 2 ∧
      matrixRegret A Sigma.val = frob ((A - canonicalCoefficient Sigma.val) * SX) ^ 2)

Lemma F.7 / scoped manuscript result

Rescaling invariance

Proved in LeanPapers.WassersteinRegretS2S3.rescalingInvariance

Manuscript statement

Let P,Q∈P2​(Rd) and A∈A. For λ>0, let Pλ​ and Qλ​ denote the pushforwards of P and Q, respectively, under z↦λz. We then have
W2​(Pλ​,Qλ​)​=λW2​(P,Q),​
L(A,P_λ) =λ^2L(A,P),
R(A,P_λ) =λ^2R(A,P),
κP_λ =κP. Moreover, for ε≥0, Rλε⋆​(Pλ​)=λ2Rε⋆​(P), and the optimization problems defining DROλε​[Pλ​] and DROε​[P] have the same minimizer set.

Convention made explicit. This result carries the Euclidean-norm convention used by the manuscript. The formalization proves that convention in assPin1_valid. The singular-value convention is proved in assSingularValueConvention_valid. Both are included in the axiom audit.

Formal statement

Exact signature from the verified source. Open the proof to follow each dependency.

lemma rescalingInvariance {p m : ℕ} [NeZero p] [NeZero m]
    (hpin : assPin1 m) (hsv : assSingularValueConvention) (P Q : JointLaws p m)
    (A : Mat m p) (a : ℝ) (ha : 0 < a) :
    wassersteinDistance (dilateLaw a P) (dilateLaw a Q) = a * wassersteinDistance P Q ∧
    risk A (dilateLaw a P) = a ^ 2 * risk A P ∧
    regret A (dilateLaw a P) = a ^ 2 * regret A P ∧
    conditionNumber (dilateLaw a P) = conditionNumber P ∧
    (∀ eps : ℝ≥0,
      localMinimax (dilateLaw a P) (a.toNNReal * eps) = ENNReal.ofReal (a ^ 2) * localMinimax P
        eps ∧
      populationMinimizers 1 (dilateLaw a P) (a.toNNReal * eps) = populationMinimizers 1 P eps)

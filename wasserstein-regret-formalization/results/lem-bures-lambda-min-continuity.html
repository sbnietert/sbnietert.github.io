Bures continuity of the smallest feature eigenvalue

Proved in LeanPapers.WassersteinRegretS2S3.buresLambdaMinContinuity

Manuscript statement

For Σ,Σˉ∈S+d​, we have
​λmin​(ΣˉX​)​−λmin​(ΣX​)​​≤B(Σˉ,Σ).
In particular, λmin​(ΣX​) is continuous with respect to the Bures metric.

Formal statement

Exact signature from the verified source. Open the proof to follow each dependency.

lemma buresLambdaMinContinuity (hsv : assSingularValueConvention)
    {p m : ℕ} [NeZero p] [NeZero m] :
    (∀ Sigma barSigma : PSD (p + m),
      |Real.sqrt (lambdaMin (blockX p m barSigma.val)) -
        Real.sqrt (lambdaMin (blockX p m Sigma.val))| ≤ buresDistance barSigma.val Sigma.val) ∧
    (∀ Sigma : PSD (p + m), ∀ eta : ℝ, 0 < eta → ∃ delta : ℝ, 0 < delta ∧
      ∀ barSigma : PSD (p + m), buresDistance barSigma.val Sigma.val < delta →
        |lambdaMin (blockX p m barSigma.val) - lambdaMin (blockX p m Sigma.val)| < eta)
